Sparkle Time — put it on an iPad
================================

1. Unzip this folder.
2. AirDrop the whole folder to the iPad (or send with Files / iCloud Drive).
3. On the iPad open the Files app, tap index.html.
4. If it opens in Files preview, tap Share (square with arrow) and choose Safari, or tap and hold and Open in Safari.
5. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

The star icon appears on the home screen. It works without internet after that first open.

Keep icon-180.png and the art folder next to index.html. Do not separate them.
